## October 2026

- **Dropdown Menu.** Springs open from its trigger, with one highlight gliding between items for both pointer and keyboard.
- **Fluid Highlight** now rests on an `active` item when the pointer leaves, and follows it through layout changes.
- **MotionSpeedProvider** slows every kinematic spring inside it, for tuning and demos.
- Docs: Markdown for every page, `llms.txt`, and a Copy page menu.

## September 2026

- First release: Button, Tabs, Switch, Fluid Highlight and the motion presets.
